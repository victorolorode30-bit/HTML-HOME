Step-by-Step Procedure For Preparing Jollof Rice
Step 1
Turn on the gas and put a pot on fire
Step 2
Prepare the pepper mix
Step 3
Parboil the rice
Step 4
Fry the base
Step 5
Cook the pepper mix/dd>
Step 6
Add stock and rice
Step 7
Steam the rice
Step 8
Drop the pot off the gas and serve
